About AMS Prime Egypt

We are an export house in Dokki, Giza, founded in 2026. We ship mainly to Kuwait, and also to Saudi Arabia and the UAE, across nine categories from animal feed to furniture made to order.

See what we export

The company

AMS Prime Egypt For Trading and Export began with animal feed: alfalfa and wheat hay, barley and malt, cotton seed and horse feed. Today we export nine categories, from frozen and fresh produce to olives, chocolate, beverages and grocery, household goods and classical furniture made to order. We deal with producers and suppliers directly and run each export ourselves, from the quotation to the loading. For other ports we work through forwarding partners.
