02 Frozen vegetables

Frozen vegetables

Fifteen vegetable lines, individually quick frozen (IQF) in Egypt: every piece is frozen on its own, so the product stays free-flowing and you use what you need and keep the rest frozen.

Broccoli is one of our key export lines. Vegetables are packed in retail bags or bulk export cartons, to your specification, and shipped at -18 °C in refrigerated containers. All grades and qualities are supplied to your specification. The range suits importers, distributors, retail packers and food service buyers who want one supplier for several frozen lines in one load.
